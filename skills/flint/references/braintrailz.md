# Braintrailz Composition Notes: Flint

Why this skill is shaped this way. Not a changelog.

## The scaffold came before the skill

Marc asked for a project like an existing tool, but insisted on project, terms, spec, plan and design before code. The method was already present across his projects. This session named and packaged it.

The five files are not the discovery. The separation of purpose, behavior, vocabulary, delivery and design makes another participant's judgment less dependent on the conversation that produced them.

## One trail, not another planning application

Keep the center in ordinary files. Participants and hosts change; original intent, evidence and decisions should remain readable.

This follows [Finding / Founding BrainTrailz](https://braintrailz.com/blog/finding-founding-braintrailz): the trail belongs to the work, not to one chat.

## Trailcard is the foundation

[Intent Is Not Enough](https://braintrailz.com/blog/intent-is-not-enough) distinguishes acceptable outcome, authority, judgment, action and evidence. [Trailcard](https://github.com/braintrailz/trailcard) already implements that contract.

Flint uses it. Duplicating it would create two competing versions of the boundary.

## The method had to accept correction

During its development, a native agent interface was questioned because the TUI already ran in the editor. That comparison missed complementary functionality, visual experience and continuity. A concrete multi-file approval problem made the gap visible.

The lesson generalizes: a feature checklist does not establish experience equivalence. The opposite also holds: a compelling experience does not establish that a supported integration can deliver it.

The reusable examples are synthetic. The private source projects are not part of this package.

## Documentation can become the delay

A skill that prevents premature coding can become another way never to begin. That happened close enough to this work to be a design constraint.

Start with the smallest useful decision. Unknowns block the commitments that depend on them, not all thought or already-authorized work. Keep questions consequential and documents proportionate.

## The refusal condition

Stop, defer and rescope are legitimate outputs. So is proceeding directly with a small slice when authority and evidence are sufficient.

Never manufacture a scaffold to make a stopped project look productive.

## Leave proof, not a victory speech

A validator can see broken links. It cannot see a bad investment hidden behind good headings. Report that limit.

The first case study is not an independent benchmark. The skill's strength must be earned through cases, fresh readers and correction, not asserted by the skill that wrote itself.
