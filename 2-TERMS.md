# Flint dictionary

**Updated:** November 19, 2026. Draft terminology; behavior is governed by [3-SPEC.md](3-SPEC.md).

## Core terms

- **Flint:** the Braintrailz project-definition companion to Trailcard. The tool that tests/shapes a spark, not the idea itself.
- **Idea/kindling:** the opportunity, known need or initial proposal brought to the process.
- **Trailcard:** the existing six-part governing rubric written before action, audited afterward.
- **Original intent:** the user's instruction preserved verbatim; not a summary rewritten by a later agent.
- **Interpretation:** a participant's understanding of intent. Label it and keep it distinct from the source.
- **Pursuit decision:** a recommendation about a named next investment, not blanket authority for the whole project.
- **Delivery envelope:** the time, scope, quality, cost, capacity, dependency and operational constraints around that investment.
- **Documentation-only scaffold:** project/terms/spec/plan/design artifacts, before executable files or dependencies.
- **Executable scaffold:** a minimal runnable structure that answers an approved question. It is not required simply because documents exist.
- **Slice:** a bounded end-to-end deliverable with relevant tests, guidance, docs and evidence.
- **Gate:** an evidence requirement or owner decision before the next investment.

## Decisions

- **Proceed:** the named next slice has sufficient justification and authority.
- **Investigate:** a bounded uncertainty needs evidence before committing.
- **Rescope:** the outcome is worthwhile but the proposed boundary/approach needs to change.
- **Defer:** timing, dependencies or capacity prevent a responsible next step.
- **Stop:** do not pursue the current proposal; preserve the reasons and useful learning.

An agent recommends; the owner authorizes within the applicable Trailcard. A Proceed decision on research is not permission to build or publish.

## Evidence and requirements

- **Known need:** a need the user says exists. Not automatically independent evidence of market demand.
- **Feasibility:** whether an approach can work within the applicable constraints.
- **Experience value:** improvements in understanding, navigation, interaction or review that may matter even at feature parity.
- **Complementary capability:** functionality that adds to another surface rather than reproducing it.
- **Continuity:** preservation of work across participants/surfaces. Qualify transcript, backend context, files and active execution separately.
- **NFR:** a non-functional quality/constraint requirement with a target or explicit unknown, measurement, owner and gate.
- **Unknown:** not established; give it an owner, consequence and resolution gate.
- **Assumption:** a proposition used provisionally; identify the test and what fails if it is false.
- **Candidate:** an option not yet selected. Do not count candidate features as user-approved scope.
- **Evidence:** an inspectable source, artifact or observed result—not merely confident explanation.
- **Mechanical validation:** checks of structure, links and declared fields; not proof of truth, safety or feasibility.
- **Audit:** Trailcard's separation of evidenced/asserted/missing claims and boundary checks.

## Naming crosswalk

Kindling and Firstlight were proposals in this conversation; Flint supersedes them as the active companion name. Trailhead already names a different product on Braintrailz. Do not rename Trailcard, Trailcraft or Repack.

The `warp-code` case is an application during method development, not a controlled baseline experiment.
